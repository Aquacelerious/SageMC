Server Rules

Read these before you play. Breaking them can lead to a mute, kick or ban.
Questions? Open a support ticket.

Respect everyone−
  1. Be kind. No harassment, hate speech, threats or targeting of any player or staff member.
  2. Keep chat clean. No spam, caps abuse, excessive swearing or sexual content.
  3. No drama copy-pasting. Do not bring outside fights into SageMC chat.
Fair play+
  1. No hacked clients. KillAura, Reach, X-Ray texture packs and auto-clickers are strictly banned.
  2. Allowed mods. Sodium, Optifine, MiniMaps without cave/entity radar, and ArmorStatus HUDs are fine.
Survival and Hardcore+
  1. No griefing claimed land. Bypassing claim borders with TNT cannons or pistons is bannable.
  2. Keep lag machines off. Massive redstone clocks without an on/off lever will be removed.
PvP and Minigames+
  1. No cross-teaming. Teaming in Solo BedWars or Free-For-All arenas results in a temporary ban.
  2. No combat logging. Disconnecting mid-fight forfeits your inventory automatically.
Skins, names and items+
  1. Keep skins appropriate. Offensive skins or renamed items with slurs will be confiscated.
Store and purchases+
  1. All store items are cosmetic or server perks. Chargebacks result in an automatic network ban.
Staff and appeals+
  1. Ban appeals. Open a support ticket with your IGN and ban ID within 14 days.
